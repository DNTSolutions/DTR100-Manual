# Safety Warning

**Read and follow all safety instructions before operating the grinder.**  
Failure to do so may result in serious injury from rotating parts, dust inhalation, or electrical shock.

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Always wear:
- Safety glasses or face shield.
- Dust mask or respirator (P2 filter minimum).
- Hearing protection if operating multiple units.
- Long sleeves and gloves (non-flammable).

## Machine Hazards

- **Rotating grinder wheel**: Keep hands, clothing, and loose items away.
- **Tungsten dust**: Toxic if inhaled; use in well-ventilated area.
- **Electrical**: Disconnect power before maintenance.
- **Sharp electrodes**: Handle inserted tungsten with care.

## Prohibited Actions

- Do not operate without guards in place.
- Do not exceed maximum electrode diameter (3.2 mm).
- Do not use damaged or worn grinding wheels.
- Never leave machine unattended while powered on.

**Stop immediately** if unusual noise, vibration, or odor occurs.[cite:40]

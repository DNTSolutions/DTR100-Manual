# Insider Guide

Pro tips for maximizing performance and longevity.

## Advanced Adjustments

- **Wheel Speed**: Factory 10,000 RPM optimal; reduce to 8,000 RPM for fine grinding (internal pot adjustment).
- **Collet Tension**: Overtighten slightly for thin electrodes (<1.6 mm).
- **Dust Management**: Connect 40 mm vacuum hose to outlet for zero downtime.

## Calibration

1. Measure 10 known 2.4 mm electrodes.
2. Adjust parallelism screw if variance >0.05 mm.
3. Verify angle with digital protractor.

## Optimization

- Batch grind 50+ electrodes per wheel side.
- Use coolant mist for >500/hour production.
- Store at 20-25°C, <60% humidity.

Common insider mod: Add foot pedal for hands-free cycling.[cite:40]
